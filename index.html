Policy

How Veyzen Wallet Bot handles your information with care and transparency.

01Introduction

Effective date: October 21, 2026. This policy explains how Veyzen Wallet Bot handles information when you use it.

02Information stored

The Bot may store your Discord user ID and information you save: public wallet addresses, address-book entries, optional PayPal handle, preferences, custom messages, reminders, price alerts, and feedback.

The Bot does not collect private keys, seed phrases, passwords, recovery phrases, or payment-card information. Do not send such information to the Bot.

03Use and feedback

Saved information is used only for requested features. With /feedback, your message and Discord user information are sent privately to the configured developer.

04Third parties and deletion

The Bot uses Discord and public market, blockchain, fee, QR-code, and news providers. You can remove saved data through Bot commands; contact @.vyzn. for other deletion requests.

05Security and contact

Reasonable measures protect stored data, but no service guarantees absolute security. Questions can be sent to @.vyzn. on Discord.
